# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

This project is meant to be a welcoming place to share small programs and
experiments. Everyone taking part is expected to help keep it that way,
regardless of experience level, background or identity.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and assume good faith.
- Give feedback on the code, not the person who wrote it.
- Accept that maintainers may decline a contribution.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults or personal attacks.
- Publishing others' private information.
- Sustained disruption of discussions or review.

## Reporting

If you see behaviour that breaks these expectations, open an issue or contact
the maintainer directly. Reports will be reviewed and handled as privately as
the situation allows.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions — and
when representing the project elsewhere.
